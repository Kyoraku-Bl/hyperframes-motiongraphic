# The Midnight Search — build spec

**Angle:** Loss aversion — the sale you never knew you lost
**Business:** Sharp & Sons Barbershop
**Hook:** HE NEEDED A CUT / AT 11PM / BOOKED NEXT DOOR.

House style is locked in `STYLE.md`; this file only records what makes this
Reel different. 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 18.000 s · no music · word-by-word
captions throughout · clean loop back to the opening plate.

## Shot script
- **0.00–3.00 Hook.** Camera already moving at frame 1, hook line on screen
  from frame 0. Opening plate: `b1-barber.jpg`.
- **3.00–8.00 Pitch.** Laptop opens, owner sceptical, drawn website revealed
  with real prices and a WhatsApp action, push-in on the owner's realisation.
- **8.00–13.00 Proof.** Phone mini-site, notification toast, busy shop,
  handshake that becomes a hug.
- **13.00–18.00 CTA.** Selfie angle, "Your website. Done for you.", loop-back
  to the exact opening plate + hook line.

## Dialogue
- `a-open` 0.15–1.35s — Next door. Sorry.
- `a-angle` 1.40–5.60s — He searched barber near me at eleven. No site, so Google sent him two blocks down.
- `a-react` 5.70–7.40s — Wait. I lost a client in my sleep?
- `a-vo` 8.10–11.55s — A simple, fast website and customers start coming to you.
- `a-thanks` 11.57–13.02s — Thanks, found you online!
- `a-cta` 13.05–16.50s — Want a website for your business? DM FREE to get your free mockup.

## Why it creates the need
Someone needed a haircut at 11pm last night. He searched "barber near me". Your shop wasn't there. Google sent him two blocks down. 💈

The viewer is shown a concrete loss they can picture happening to their own
business tonight, then shown the one small thing that prevents it. The CTA is
a free mockup, so acting costs nothing.
